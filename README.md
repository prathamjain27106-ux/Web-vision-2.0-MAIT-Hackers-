## 🚨 Problem Statement

e-NAM was created to connect agricultural markets across India and enable transparent price discovery. However, **access to market prices does not necessarily mean farmers can identify where they will earn the most from their produce.**

A farmer may see a higher price in another mandi but struggle to account for transportation, market charges and other expenses before deciding where to sell.

A CAG audit of e-NAM implementation in West Bengal highlighted this gap:

* Only **54% of commodity arrivals** in the audited markets were e-traded.
* Inter-market trading accounted for just **0.4%** of the relevant commodity volume.

*These are historical, state-specific audit findings, not current nationwide statistics.*

### Our Innovation — e-NAM 2.0

We are redesigning the existing e-NAM platform to make agricultural trading more accessible, understandable and decision-oriented.

Our key innovation, **Mandi Compare**, enables farmers to compare buyer offers based on estimated net returns rather than just quoted prices.

### Key Advantages

1. **Smarter Selling Decisions:** Compare different mandis and identify which offer provides a better estimated return.
2. **Cost Transparency:** Account for transportation and other charges before choosing a market.
3. **Simplified Experience:** Access important agricultural services through a cleaner, more intuitive interface.
4. **Better Accessibility:** Improve mobile usability and simplify complex trading workflows.
5. **Transaction Clarity:** Understand auction progress, trade details and payment status more easily.

**Our goal is simple: Help farmers focus on what they actually earn, not just what the market offers.**
